# Eyecare Accounts Receivable Management

Your practice earned it. Are you collecting all of it?

[IMG alt="image"]

[IMG alt="image"]

You can run a full schedule, fill the optical, and submit every claim, and still end the month unsure how much of that work has actually turned into cash. The gap between care delivered and money collected is where good practices quietly lose ground. Accounts receivable management closes it.
This is not another stack of reports to file away. It is a disciplined, eyecare-specific system for tracking every dollar your practice is owed: open insurance and vision plan claims, unpaid patient balances, and everything aging in between. We keep your receivables accurate, visible, and moving, so you always know what is coming in and when.

## From Aging Claims to Collected Cash

You went to school to care for eyes, not to decode an explanation of benefits or work an aging bucket between patients. So claims drift past their follow-up window, patient statements go out late, and a packed schedule can quietly mask a collections problem underneath it.
That is where we come in. ForEyes accounts receivable management gives your practice a living, accurate picture of every dollar owed to you, sorted the way eyecare actually bills. You stay with your patients. We stay on your receivables.
A quick distinction: our A/R Management lives inside your accounting, where the job is accuracy, visibility, and [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/bookkeeping] clean books. The hands-on work of submitting and appealing claims is handled by our Billing and Revenue Cycle Management service. The two are built to work hand in hand, and accurate receivables data makes both sharper.
[IMG alt="Edge Decorator"]

## Our Accounts Receivable Management Services

[IMG alt=""]

### A/R Aging and Monthly Reporting

An aging report only helps if you can read it. We organize every open balance by age, payer, and patient, then deliver a clear monthly report that puts your days in A/R right at the top. You leave every month knowing your numbers, not hunting for them in a spreadsheet. If your aging report keeps climbing, our Smart Reads breakdown of [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/blog/why-accounts-receivable-is-growing] why accounts receivable grows and how to bring it back under control walks through the usual culprits.

[IMG alt=""]

### Insurance and Vision Plan Payment Matching

Optical, vision plan, and medical claims each behave differently, and they rarely pay the way you expect. We match incoming payments to the claims they belong to, confirm you were paid what you were owed, and keep your books accurate to the dollar.

[IMG alt=""]

### Patient Balance Tracking

Small patient balances add up fast across a full schedule, and they are the easiest dollars to lose. We keep patient receivables organized and current, so you can see what is outstanding and nothing quietly falls through the cracks.

[IMG alt=""]

### Denials and Underpayment Flags

A denied or short-paid claim is money you already earned sitting just out of reach. We surface denials and underpayments as they appear and route them to [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/billing-services] Billing and Revenue Cycle Management for follow-up, so problems surface in days instead of at year-end.

[IMG alt=""]

### Days in A/R and Cash Flow Improvement

Profit on paper does not always mean money in the account. We track how quickly your receivables convert to cash, flag the tight stretches before they arrive, and give you a dependable read on the money moving toward your practice. We also name the levers: which aging buckets to work first, where your follow-up cadence is slipping, and which payers consistently take the longest to pay. Those three answers are where most eyecare practices recover cash without adding a single patient to the schedule.

[IMG alt=""]

### Collections Priorities and Follow-Up Support

Not every unpaid balance deserves the same urgency. We rank your open balances by age, size, and likelihood of collection, then tell your team exactly which claims and patient accounts to work this week and in what order. When the work calls for hands-on submission, appeals, or collections calls, it moves to Billing and Revenue Cycle Management with the priority list already built.

[IMG alt=""]

### Month-End Close and Secure Portal Delivery

We close your [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/bookkeeping] books by the 10th business day of every month and deliver your receivables reports through your secure client portal. Current, dependable, and ready whenever you are.

## What Sets Our A/R Management Apart

[IMG alt=""]

### We Know Where Eyecare Dollars Get Stuck

Founded by Wade Weisz, OD, ForEyes has spent more than 25 years watching how eyecare revenue actually moves: the vision plan that pays late, the medical claim that comes back short, the frames sale that never got posted. We manage your receivables with a practicing owner's instinct for where money hides, not a generalist's checklist.

[IMG alt=""]

### Receivables Read Through an Eyecare Lens

Optical, medical, and vision plan dollars each follow their own rules, and a general accountant lumps them into one line. Our custom chart of accounts keeps them separate on your books, so your aging report reflects how an eyecare practice really earns and shows exactly where your cash is tied up.

[IMG alt=""]

### A Second Set of Eyes on Every Number

Nothing reaches you unchecked. A quality control manager reviews your receivables reporting before it lands in your portal, and the workflow around it, [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/accounting] Accounts Payable through Bill.com and Payroll through Gusto, runs with the same certified discipline. Backed by Certified Support, accuracy is the standard and not the exception.

[IMG alt=""]

### Less Follow-Up on Your Plate

Chasing balances and decoding remittances is exactly the work that swallows an owner's evenings. We take the tracking, the matching, and the flagging off your desk, so your team can spend its energy on patients at the front desk instead of on the aging report.
