## About foreyes

## Built by an OD Who Understands Your Challenges

After 25 years of running a successful private practice, founder Wade Weisz, OD, understood a fundamental truth: accurate financials are essential to a thriving eyecare business—but tracking them shouldn't take time away from patient care.

That insight led to the founding of ForEyes Solutions in 2021, with a clear mission: to help fellow eyecare professionals take control of their practice finances.

Today, ForEyes Solutions is a trusted partner for forward-thinking optometrists, ophthalmologists, and practice owners across the country—helping them simplify operations, trust their numbers, and focus on what matters most: exceptional patient care.

What truly sets us apart is the industry expertise within our team. With Wade’s background as an OD and staff who have spent 15+ years running and managing eyecare practices, we understand the operational, financial, and strategic realities that come with ownership.

With our robust accounting solutions and fully integrated practice solutions, we act as an extension of your practice. We help you operate more efficiently, make confident financial decisions, and reclaim your time—so you can do what you do best: care for your patients.

## Our Services

## Comprehensive Back-Office Solutions

Everything you need to run a financially healthy practice, all from one trusted partner.

### Accounting Solutions

[IMG alt="None"]

Bookkeeping

Stay on top of your financials without the late nights. Our team manages day-to-day transaction entry, reconciliations, and financial reporting with accuracy and timeliness—giving you clear, reliable books you can actually trust.

[IMG alt="None"]

Tax Services

From proactive tax planning to year-end filings, our CPA specializes in the eyecare industry. You’ll get strategies to minimize tax liability, stay compliant, and make smarter financial decisions—all with professionals who understand your business model inside and out.

[IMG alt="None"]

Accounting

Our accounting service goes beyond the basics to cover two of the biggest operational pain points for practices: payroll and accounts payable. We ensure your staff are paid accurately and on time, taxes are filed properly, and vendor bills are managed with efficiency and transparency.

### Practice Solutions

[IMG alt="None"]

Practice Consulting

Running a practice takes more than patient care. We advise on operations, staffing, and growth strategies—helping you streamline workflows, strengthen team performance, and position your practice for long-term success.

[IMG alt="None"]

Revenue Cycle Management

Improve cash flow and reduce write-offs with systems built for eyecare billing. We handle claim submission, tracking, and denial management to ensure you get paid faster and more consistently—so revenue isn’t left on the table.

[IMG alt="None"]

Virtual Assistant Staffing

Free yourself from administrative bottlenecks with trained virtual assistants who understand eyecare. From scheduling and patient communications to optometric scribes, we provide reliable staff extensions that reduce overhead while keeping your practice running smoothly.
