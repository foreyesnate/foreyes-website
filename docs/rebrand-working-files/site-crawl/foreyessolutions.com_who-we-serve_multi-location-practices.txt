## Spend Less Time on Admin, More on Patients

Managing a single eyecare office is challenging, but running multiple locations multiplies the complexity. Payroll, [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/bookkeeping] bookkeeping, [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/billing-services] billing, and operational oversight can quickly consume your time and energy, taking your attention away from patient care and strategic growth.
ForEyes Solutions provides scalable bookkeeping and accounting for multi-location practices, as well as comprehensive practice solutions, giving you actionable insights across every location. With us, you can make confident decisions without getting bogged down in the numbers.

## Common Challenges for Growing Practices

As practices expand, new obstacles emerge. Here are the main challenges we help multi-group eyecare practices overcome.

[IMG alt=""]

Administrative Overload & Precious Time Lost

Adding location often multiplies day-to-day tasks, from payroll and accounts payable to billing follow-ups. We take these time-consuming administrative tasks off your plate, freeing up your staff while ensuring consistency across your eyecare practices.

[IMG alt=""]

Fragmented Financial Across Locations

Different processes, teams, or systems at each of your locations make it difficult to see the full picture. We provide centralized reporting and separate profit and loss statements for each location, giving you a clear view of performance–all without needing to manually consolidate numbers.

[IMG alt=""]

Unclear Profitability & Owner Compensation

Revenue may look strong across locations, but that doesn’t always mean profits (or personal income) are where they should be. Between varying expenses at each clinic, W-2 wages and owner draws, it’s easy to lose track of what you’re actually earning. We break down profitability by location and clarify your true take-home pay, so you know exactly which clinic contributes to the bottom line–and what ends up in your pocket.

[IMG alt=""]

Difficulty Benchmarking Performance

Without standardized metrics, it’s difficult to know which locations are thriving and which need attention. Our systems create consistent, actionable reporting to help you identify trends, compare results, and make data-driven decisions.

[IMG alt=""]

Chaos While Scaling

From inefficiencies to redundant workflows, opening new locations also opens you up to costly mistakes. We implement scalable systems from day one, so growth is smooth, predictable, and profitable.

## Accounting, Advisory & Practice Solutions Services

### Bookkeeping

When bookkeeping for multiple clinics, it’s easy to feel in the dark about performance. Our assistance gives you reliable profit and loss statements for each location, helping focus your resources. No more second-guessing–just decisions backed by clear data.[LINK→/services/bookkeeping] Get Your Books in Order

### Accounting

Managing payroll and payables across several locations can take hours each month–hours that, as a business owner, you don’t have. By teaming up with us for multi-location practice accounting, you get on-time payments for staff and vendors at every location. This means less admin stress and more energy for running each practice.[LINK→/services/accounting] Free Yourself From Admin Burdens

### Tax Services

Continuing to scale your practice shouldn’t come with unpredictable tax bills. Our experts provide proactive planning and strategy year-round, helping you minimize liability and protect cash flow across all locations. Head into tax season confidently, knowing all deductions are maximized and that each location has been set up for success.[LINK→/services/tax-services] Grow Without Tax Surprises

### Practice Consulting

Expanding doesn’t always equal earning more, and with only optometry group financial services, it’s difficult to know why you’re falling short–you need practice consulting assistance for that. With deep OD and practice management experience, ForEyes Solutions pinpoints inefficiencies in systems, staffing, and operations, then delivers strategies that increase profitability and free up time for leadership teams. You’ll finally feel like your growth is paying off.[LINK→/services/practice-consulting] Get a Roadmap to Profitability

### Billing Services

The more eyecare practices you run, the more difficult billing gets–and the easier it is for revenue to slip through the cracks. Our specialists handle claims, denials, and patient billing across all of your locations, resulting in fewer delays and less billing stress.[LINK→/services/billing-services] Stop Chasing Payments
