# Accounting & Advisory Support Exclusively for Optometry Practices

You became an optometrist to care for patients–not to spend late nights reconciling receipts and second-guessing your financials. That's where we come in. At ForEyes Solutions, we provide independent [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/practice-consulting] optometry practices with the support, systems, and financial clarity needed to grow confidently.
Founded by an OD who has spent 25+ years running successful practices, we don’t just talk the talk–we’ve walked the walk. Today, we're a go-to accounting advisory, and solution partner for forward-thinking eyecare practices across the country–and we're ready to help you trust your numbers and reclaim your time.

## About Us

ForEyes Solutions was born from firsthand experience, because no one understands the challenges of running an eyecare practice quite like an optometrist.
Founder Wade Weisz, OD, has over 25 years of experience managing successful eye care practices, but always struggled with the same issue: accounting, practice solutions, and advisory support that didn't truly reflect the business or help him make informed decisions. So, Wade built a firm explicitly designed for eyecare professionals, where industry expertise meets modern systems and personalized assistance.
For us, comprehensive tax services, tax, accounting, and advisory support is about more than numbers–it’s about your practice’s future. We're here to help you see it clearly.[LINK→/about-us] More About Us
