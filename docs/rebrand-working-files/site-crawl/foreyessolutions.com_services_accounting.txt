## Where Bookkeeping Ends and Financial Management Begins

Bookkeeping records what happened. Accounting manages how money moves next: which vendors get paid when, how payroll costs show up against revenue, and what the numbers mean for the months ahead. We handle that layer for practices at every stage, from an [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/who-we-serve/startups] eyecare startup setting up its first approval workflow to established groups tightening cash flow. If you also want the records side covered, our [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/bookkeeping] bookkeeping services pair naturally with this work.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Accounts Payable (AP)

Every vendor bill follows the same path: entered, coded, approved by you, and paid on the date that protects your cash and your supplier relationships. We run that path exclusively through Bill.com, so nothing depends on a paper trail or a forwarded email. Under the accrual method, an expense counts when you become liable for it rather than when you pay it, which is why [LINK→https://www.irs.gov/publications/p538] payable timing matters to your reports. When a vendor might actually be a worker, we flag it, because [LINK→https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-self-employed-or-employee] classification rules decide what you owe and report.
- Approval on your terms: You review and approve in Bill.com. We handle entry, coding, and scheduling.
- Vendor-term awareness: We track due dates and payment terms with frame, lab, and supply vendors so bills are paid on time and cash is not tied up early.
- A clear audit trail: Each approval and payment is recorded in one place, so questions about what was paid, and when, have answers.
- Certified in Bill.com: Our team is certified in the platform, so your practice uses features most offices never turn on.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Payroll

Payroll is also an accounting question: what does your team really cost, and what is left for you? We run payroll through Gusto and make sure each pay run, deposit, and deduction lands in the right account, so labor cost shows up cleanly in your P&L and your own take-home pay is never a guess. For setup, filings, and benefits detail, see our [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/payroll] payroll page.
- Labor cost visibility: Wages, employer taxes, and benefits post to separate categories, so you can see payroll as a share of revenue.
- Owner pay clarity: We keep W-2 wages and owner draws separate, so your true take-home pay is unmistakable.
- Gusto-certified support: Our team works inside the platform, so you do not have to train staff on new software.
- Reports you can use: Payroll summaries feed your monthly financials and tax planning without re-keying.

### Approve, Don't Administrate

On payables you review and approve. On payroll you choose how involved to be. Either way, you keep visibility and control without the data entry.
