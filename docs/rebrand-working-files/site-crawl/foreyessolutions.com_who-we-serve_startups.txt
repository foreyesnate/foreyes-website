## Launch Confidently

Starting a new optometry or eyecare practice is exciting, but it can also be overwhelming. Between choosing a location, hiring staff, and attracting patients, daily operational needs can drain both your time and your energy. And mismanaged bookkeeping, unclear cash flow, and inefficient systems can stall growth before your practice even opens. That’s where ForEyes Solutions comes in.
We partner with new optometry practices to handle the numbers, systems, and strategy, so you can focus on building a profitable practice from day one.

## The Obstacles Most Startup Practices Encounter

[IMG alt=""]

Too much time spent on admin

Eyecare professionals often find themselves juggling invoices, payroll, and optometry startup [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/bookkeeping] bookkeeping tasks, which leaves little time for addressing operational needs and attending to patients. We handle the back-office work, freeing you up to focus on building your client base and reputation.

[IMG alt=""]

Entity setup confusion

Should you choose an S-Corp? Partnership? LLC? These decisions impact taxes, liability, and how you pay yourself. Our accounting experts guide you through entity setup and long-term tax planning, ensuring your practice starts on solid ground.

[IMG alt=""]

Unclear profitability & owner compensation

As a startup eyecare practice, it’s tough to know how much money you’re really making–or how much you can safely pay yourself. We break down revenue, expenses, and owner draws to offer a clear picture of your practice’s profitability.

[IMG alt=""]

Insurance and billing roadblocks

Insurance denials and patient billing delays put your revenue at risk–revenue that’s crucial in the early days of a startup. We manage your entire billing cycle, improving clean claim rates and keeping cash flow steady.

[IMG alt=""]

Unexpected practice challenges

Changing regulations, staffing issues, and vendor contracts can catch new business owners off guard. Our practice consulting services provide the guidance you need to navigate challenges before they turn into major setbacks.

## Our Full-Service Startup Support

### Bookkeeping

Start your eyecare practice with confidence, knowing every transaction is accurately recorded and categorized. Our specialized bookkeeping for startups ensures you have up-to-date financials, making tax prep, cash flow tracking, and profitability analysis simple and stress-free. With our detailed reports, you’ll always know exactly where your practice stands.[LINK→/services/bookkeeping] Be Confident in Your Numbers

### Accounting

It’s not enough to record your numbers–you have to understand them. Our new practice accounting services deliver timely, actionable insights, from monthly profit and loss statements to balance sheets and reconciliations. You’ll make smarter decisions, track growth more effectively, and avoid surprises, all while saving hours of administrative work each week.[LINK→/services/accounting] Interpret the Data

### Tax Services

Taxes shouldn’t be a guessing game, especially for new practice owners. Our tax experts provide planning, strategy, and preparation for both business and personal returns, ensuring you optimize deductions and minimize liabilities. You focus on patients–we’ll handle the complex tax strategy and compliance needs.[LINK→/services/tax-services] Plan for Success

### Billing

Revenue is the lifeblood of your new optometry or eyecare practice. Our billing experts manage claims submission, payment posting, denial follow-ups, and even patient billing inquiries, so you get paid faster and with fewer headaches. By reducing errors and improving cash flow, you’ll be able to concentrate on building your patient base.[LINK→/services/practice-consulting] Claim Control

### Practice Consulting

Starting a practice comes with decisions that affect profitability for years. Our consulting team–led by a practicing OD–helps you optimize everything from operations and inventory to your financial strategy. We solve real-world challenges using real-world experience, offering industry-specific guidance that allows you to grow without the trial-and-error phase.[LINK→/services/billing-services] Get Industry-Specific Guidance

## How We Work

- Discovery call: We learn about where you’re starting from, as well as your short- and long-term goals.
- Kickoff and onboarding: Meet your onboarding specialist and get set up in your client portal.
- Foundation setup: We structure your systems, organize your financials, and prepare you for growth.
- Ongoing support: From monthly reports to strategic guidance and everything in between, we keep your books accurate and your practice on track.
- Annual review: Each year, we revisit your performance and plan for what’s ahead.
