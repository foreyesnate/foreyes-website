### Accounting

Routine tasks like payroll and vendor payments can quickly eat into your schedule and distract you from patient care. Our accounting services free you up from the day-to-day while ensuring your team and vendors are always paid accurately and on time. With consistent, reliable processes in place, you won’t have to chase paperwork or worry about errors disrupting operations–you’ll simply know the details have been handled.[LINK→/services/accounting] Outsource Your Accounting

### Bookkeeping

Disorganized or incomplete books leave many practice owners feeling like they’re flying blind. Our industry-specific bookkeeping services bring clarity to your financials. Each month, you’ll receive easy-to-read profit and loss statements built around a custom chart of accounts. The result? Confidence in your numbers, insight into performance, and the data you need to make more informed decisions.[LINK→/services/bookkeeping] Streamline Your Books

### Tax Services

Tax time shouldn’t come with surprise bills. Our tax services for eyecare professionals give you a year-round tax strategy, proactive planning, and compliant filings. Whether it’s your business or personal returns, our certified experts minimize liability while maximizing cash flow. Connect with us for a tax strategy that aligns with your growth goals.[LINK→/services/tax-services] Create a Proactive Tax Plan

### Consulting

Working hard but not seeing that effort reflected in your bottom line? Our consulting team, led by a practicing OD, dives deep into your operations, evaluating everything from inventory and staffing to financial systems and optical processes. Because we’ve been in your shoes, we can identify inefficiencies, growth opportunities, and create a profitable path forward better than most. It’s like having a de facto COO–without the overhead.[LINK→/services/practice-consulting] Solve Roadblocks

[IMG alt="mobile-image"]

[IMG alt="desktop-image"]

### Medical & Vision Billing Services

Insurance billing is one of the biggest time drains in any eyecare office, and mistakes can leave money on the table. Our certified billing specialists handle claims submission, denial management, A/R follow-up, and patient billing with an eyecare-specific focus. By increasing your clean claim rate and reducing days in A/R, we help you collect what you’ve earned, faster. And because we integrate billing with financial reporting, you get a complete picture of your revenue cycle, from first patient visit to final payment, all without managing an in-house biller.[LINK→/services/billing-services] Take Billing Off Your Plate
