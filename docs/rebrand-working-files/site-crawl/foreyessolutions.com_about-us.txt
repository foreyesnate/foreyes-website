# About ForEyes Solutions

We specialize in accounting services exclusively for the eyecare industry–optometry, ophthalmology, and optical. Founded by an OD with decades of experience, our mission is straightforward: to help you gain control over your practice’s finances with accurate, reliable data that helps you make smarter decisions, faster.

## Our (20/20) Vision

To become the leading accounting, advisory, and practice solutions partner for the eyecare industry, trusted for our expertise, integrity, and unwavering support.

## A Word From Wade Weisz, OD
ForEyes Solutions Founder

“After more than 25 years as a practicing OD and managing multiple locations, I learned firsthand how critical it is to have accurate, timely financial data–and how often that’s missing in the eyecare profession.
Too many practices operate with incomplete books, misclassified expenses, and reports that don’t tell the whole story. I’ve been there, and know how that uncertainty impacts not just your bottom line but your ability to lead, grow, and sleep at night.
I’ve lived your challenges and built this company to solve them, with accuracy you can trust and support that feels like part of your team.”

## How It All Started

Our firm was born from a simple idea: What if there was an accounting, advisory, and practice solutions partner that truly understood the eyecare industry?
What began as a small team led by an OD serving a handful of independent optometrists has grown into a specialized accounting and advisory firm trusted by practices across the country. Our growth has been intentional, rooted in a deep understanding of the unique financial challenges that come with running a practice.
Today, we support our clients with customized services, from bookkeeping and taxes to tax services and advisory support. Why? So you can focus on what matters most: delivering exceptional patient care.
