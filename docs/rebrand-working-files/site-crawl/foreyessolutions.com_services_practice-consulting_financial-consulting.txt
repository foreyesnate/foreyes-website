## From Numbers to a Plan You Can Use

Most ODs were trained to care for patients, not to interpret a Profit and Loss (P&L) statement between exams. So the financials pile up, the questions grow, and big decisions get made on instinct instead of evidence.
We change that. ForEyes Financial Consulting gives you a partner who sits in the owner's seat with you, reads the story your numbers are telling, and hands you a clear, prioritized path forward. You keep your focus on the chair. We keep the financial strategy in focus.
Our [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/practice-consulting] Practice Consulting service looks across the whole practice: operations, staffing, workflow, and financial performance. Financial Consulting goes deeper on the money itself, with dedicated work on cash flow, margins, compensation, valuation, and the forward plan that ties them together.
[IMG alt="Edge Decorator"]

## What We Handle for Your Practice

[IMG alt=""]

### Financial Statement Interpretation and KPI Reporting

A P&L only helps if you can read it. We sit with you and walk through what each line is really saying, then build a short list of the metrics that actually move your practice: revenue per exam, optical capture rate, cost of goods on the optical side, and provider productivity. You leave every session knowing your numbers, not just owning them.

[IMG alt=""]

### Cash Flow Planning and Forecasting

Profit on paper does not always mean money in the account. We map how cash actually moves through your practice across the year, flag the tight months before they arrive, and build a forecast you can plan against. That means fewer surprises at payroll, smarter timing on big purchases, and a cushion you can count on. When the pressure is coming from slow reimbursements rather than spending, we point you toward [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/billing-services] faster collections as the fix.

[IMG alt=""]

### Profitability and Margin Analysis

Not all revenue is equal. We break down where your true profit comes from, separating optical margins from medical reimbursements and exam revenue, so you can see which parts of the practice carry the business and which quietly drain it. Then we help you protect the strong lines and fix the weak ones.

[IMG alt=""]

### Owner Compensation and True Take-Home Pay

Many owners cannot say, with confidence, what they actually earn. We clarify the difference between [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/accounting] W-2 wages and owner draws so your real take-home pay is unmistakable, then help you structure compensation in a way that supports both your household and the health of the practice.

[IMG alt=""]

### EBITDA and Valuation Readiness

Whether you are years from an exit or simply want to build something worth more, EBITDA is the language of practice value. We calculate it accurately for your practice, show you the levers that raise it, and keep your finances in a shape that stands up to a buyer, a partner, or a lender. For groups, that same work supports [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/who-we-serve/multi-location-practices] multi-location benchmarking so you can compare sites on equal footing.

[IMG alt=""]

### Budgeting, Growth Planning, and the Forward Roadmap

Guesswork is expensive. We build a working budget around your real numbers and your goals, then turn it into a roadmap: when you can afford the new lane, the second location, the associate hire, or the equipment upgrade. Every major move gets tested against the numbers before you commit. When you are weighing a second location, an associate, or a lane expansion, we model the scenario first so the growth decision is a calculation rather than a leap.
