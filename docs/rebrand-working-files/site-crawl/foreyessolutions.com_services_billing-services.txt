# Optometry Billing & Collections Services

[IMG alt="desktop-image"]

## Maximize Revenue, Minimize Headaches

Medical billing for eye doctors is one of the biggest administrative burdens for practices, and one of the easiest places to lose revenue. If your staff is stretched too thin or your denial rate feels too high, it’s time to put your billing needs in the hands of our specialists. Our optometry billing and collections services give you an end-to-end revenue cycle management (RCM) solution that saves time, improves cash flow, and delivers the financial clarity your eyecare practice needs.

## ForEyes Solutions Billing Services

Managing insurance billing in-house can drain valuable time, increase overhead costs, and slow down cash flow. Our insurance billing services for eyecare professionals provide a dedicated team of certified experts focused on accurate claim submission, faster reimbursements, and consistent revenue collection—without the expense of an additional in-house staff member. By keeping your financial records more organized and consistent, our process also helps support smoother accounting and [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/tax-services] tax preparation throughout the year.
Beyond simply filing claims, we optimize your entire revenue cycle management process, from patient intake and coding accuracy to payment posting, denial management, and collections, helping your practice improve profitability, reduce billing errors, and maintain healthier cash flow.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Claims Submission & Payment Posting

Every rejected claim impacts your cash flow. We meticulously review claims for accuracy before submission and post payments promptly, reducing denials and helping you get paid faster–without your team spending hours reworking claims.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Denial Management & A/R Follow-Up

Left unresolved, denials and old receivables can quietly drain revenue. Our eyecare billing specialists stay on top of insurance companies, appealing denials and following up on unpaid claims, so you can stop wasting time chasing down payments.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Patient Billing Support

Billing questions can overwhelm your staff and slow down your front desk. We handle patient billing inquiries directly, saving your team valuable time while ensuring patients get the clear, professional answers they deserve about their statements.

[IMG alt="icon"]

### Coding Audits & Compliance

General billers often miss the nuances of optometry and ophthalmology coding, leading to underpayments or compliance risks. Our certified coders conduct regular coding audits and stay current on eyecare-specific rules so you can maximize reimbursements and protect your revenue.

Billing shouldn’t drain your time or your revenue. With ForEyes Solutions, you get a dedicated team of industry experts all working to keep your practice more profitable.

### Eyecare Expertise

Unlike general medical billers, our certified team specializes in optometry and ophthalmology. We know the codes, common pitfalls, and the best practices to minimize denials and get you paid faster.

### Maximized Revenue

Our goal isn’t to simply manage your billing–it’s to improve it. We increase your clean claim rate, proactively resolve denials, and work to shorten your days in accounts receivable (A/R), ensuring you collect every dollar you’ve earned.

### Integrated Finances

When combined with our accounting solutions, you get a holistic view of your practice’s financial health, from the first patient encounter to the final payment. This comprehensive level of clarity makes it easier to plan, grow, and strategize.
