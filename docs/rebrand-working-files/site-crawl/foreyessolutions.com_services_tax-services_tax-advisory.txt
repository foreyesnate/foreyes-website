## Strategic Tax Advisory

Every major business decision has tax implications. Our tax advisory services incorporate proactive [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/tax-services/tax-planning] tax planning to help you evaluate purchases, investments, compensation strategies, and operational changes before decisions are made. By combining strategic guidance with tax planning, we help you make tax-efficient decisions that support your overall financial objectives and long-term practice growth.

[IMG alt="image"]

### Business Structure & Owner Advisory

As your practice evolves, your business structure and compensation strategy may need to evolve with it. We advise practice owners on entity selection, ownership changes, partner transitions, salary distributions, and other financial decisions that can significantly affect tax outcomes and long-term profitability.

[IMG alt="image"]

### Growth & Investment Advisory

Whether you're investing in new equipment, renovating your office, adding providers, or expanding into multiple locations, we help you understand the tax impact before moving forward. Our advisory approach allows you to make growth decisions with greater financial clarity and confidence.

### Compliance & Risk Advisory

Staying compliant is just as important as minimizing taxes. We monitor changing tax regulations, review potential compliance risks, and provide guidance that helps your practice remain compliant while avoiding unnecessary penalties, reporting issues, and costly surprises.

## Tax Advisory FAQs

Tax advisory is an ongoing service that helps business owners make informed financial decisions throughout the year. Rather than focusing only on [LINK→https://foreyessolutions.com/services/tax-services] filing tax returns, tax advisors provide strategic guidance that helps reduce tax exposure, improve compliance, and support long-term business growth.

The best time is before making significant financial decisions, such as purchasing equipment, changing your business structure, expanding your practice, bringing on partners, or planning major investments. Early guidance often creates better tax outcomes than reacting after decisions have already been made.
