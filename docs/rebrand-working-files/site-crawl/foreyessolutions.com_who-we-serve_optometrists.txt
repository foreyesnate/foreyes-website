## What’s Holding Your Practice Back?

Over time, what starts as a few side tasks, such as tax strategy, becomes a full-time distraction. Here are some of the most common issues we solve for optometrists:

[IMG alt=""]

Spending too much time on admin.

From payroll and AP to revenue cycle management, our team can tackle these tasks to free up your internal team’s time.

[IMG alt=""]

Unsure if books are accurate.

We clean up your financials and give you reporting you can actually use.

[IMG alt=""]

Not knowing if the practice is profitable.

Other accounting firms simply deliver your data. We deliver insight based on your data, which you can then leverage to make more informed business decisions.

[IMG alt=""]

Not knowing how much you take home.

Between W-2 wages and owner draws, it’s easy to confuse what the books show you with what actually lands in your bank account. We clarify your true take-home pay so you know exactly what you earn without fearing surprise taxes.

[IMG alt=""]

The tax-time and filing scramble.

Our professionals plan ahead and keep your books up to date, so you’re never caught off guard.
You deserve support that understands optometry inside and out. We’ve built our entire service model around helping optometrists succeed.

## Tailored Services

Here’s how each of our services is tailored to ODs, and how they deliver measurable impact.

### Bookkeeping

Bookkeeping for eyecare professionals is not a simple "plug and chug" task and is about more than just categorizing expenses. Partner with us to build your books around a chart of accounts made specifically for optometry practices. With us, you’ll get speed, efficiency, and monthly deliverables you can rely on.[LINK→/services/bookkeeping] Clean Your Books

### Accounting

Tired of spending hours managing payroll or writing vendor checks? Outsource to our accountants for optometrists! Our accounts payable and payroll services make sure every team member and vendor is paid on time. It’s operational peace of mind, without the admin burden.[LINK→/services/accounting] Get Stress-Free Payroll

### Tax Services

Ready for a smart strategy that reduces your tax liability, improves cash flow, and eliminates last-minute surprises? Partner with us for a plan built around your goals. From business to personal returns, our experts have you covered.[LINK→/services/tax-services] Develop a Tax Strategy

### Practice Consulting

Maybe your revenue is up, but your profits aren’t following. Or your staffing feels off, but you’re not sure how to fix it. We can analyze your operational model, identify what’s working well and what’s holding you back, and present specific strategies that drive growth.[LINK→/services/practice-consulting] Boost Profits

### Billing Services

Insurance claims are complicated, but they don’t have to derail your day. Our billing team manages your claim submissions, tracks aging AR, follows up, and keeps your cash flow consistent. We know the codes, the carriers, and how to get results.[LINK→/services/billing-services] Claim Control
